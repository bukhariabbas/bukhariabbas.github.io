Anonymized ITH examples

The NMF similarity heatmap and Kaplan–Meier curves are adapted from the author’s ongoing rhabdomyosarcoma analysis and manuscript materials, with permission for public display. They illustrate methods; they are not peer-reviewed clinical claims.

Public figures withhold original program identities and biological labels. No patient identifiers, patient-level tables, gene lists, or private identity mapping are included. Statistical values and curve shapes are retained rather than simulated.

The survival examples use two selected programs in the fusion-negative cohort with within-cohort median score splits. No tested association met BH-adjusted q < 0.05 in the manuscript audit. Selection of illustrative curves is exploratory, and both examples involve the same cohort rather than independent replication.
