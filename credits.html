Research plan illustrations

The research-trajectory figure and future-framework figure are reproduced unchanged from Syed Abbas Bukhari’s research plan, Figures 1 and 3 respectively. They also appear in the revised academic statement as Figures 1 and 2.

The trajectory illustration synthesizes complementary studies, rather than one experiment measuring every assay. The future-framework illustration is a proposed program. All points, niches, and survival curves are conceptual; its favorable, intermediate, and poor labels do not establish clinical risk groups. CITE-seq, spatial profiling, cell-fraction deconvolution, and pan-pediatric expansion remain proposed extensions.
