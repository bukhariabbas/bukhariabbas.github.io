Understanding variation.

I study how gene regulation shapes cellular states, behavior, and disease.

My work spans parental care in fish, genotype-to-phenotype relationships in honeybees, and pediatric tumor states. At the National Cancer Institute, I connect recurrent expression programs and regulatory context with questions about patient outcomes.

Honeybees: from genotype to phenotype ↗

My honeybee work connects germline variation with cellular regulon activity and behavioral differences—a genotype-to-phenotype (G2P) question.

  1. G
    Germline variation

    Inherited genetic differences; genotype–expression associations.

  2. R
    Cellular regulons

    Transcription-factor target programs and their activity across brain cell types.

  3. P
    Behavioral phenotype

    Soldier–forager differences in the context of colony aggression.

eQTL analysis and single-cell network analysis provide complementary evidence across datasets.
