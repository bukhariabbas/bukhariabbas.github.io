02Analytical leadership

Lead the analysis.

I connect scientific questions, computational execution, and biological interpretation so that a genomic analysis produces more than a set of plots.

01 / FRAME

Define the biological decision

Align the hypothesis, data, contrasts, and validation criteria with the scientific program.

02 / INTEGRATE

Build reliable molecular evidence

Integrate single-cell and bulk profiles with chromatin, regulatory, functional, and clinical evidence where available.

03 / CHALLENGE

Test competing explanations

Evaluate confounding, model assumptions, sensitivity, reproducibility, and contradictory evidence.

04 / DELIVER

Enable the next experiment

Deliver interpretable hypotheses, reusable software, documented outputs, and a clear handoff to collaborators.

03One scientific trajectory

Different systems.
A consistent question.

How does molecular regulation produce differences in phenotype? My doctoral work connected temporal transcription and chromatin regulation to behavior. Postdoctoral research extended that perspective across species and into single-cell networks.

At NCI, I apply the same systems-level reasoning to pediatric tumor heterogeneity and candidate therapeutic dependencies. The biology has changed; the commitment to interpretable mechanisms and rigorous evidence has not.

Future group and scientific independence

Regulatory programs and therapeutic vulnerabilities in pediatric cancer

My proposed group will connect the discovery of cellular states to candidate mechanisms, experimental tests, and careful evaluation of patient relevance.

Development and adaptation

Distinguish recurrent developmental regulation from tumor-specific, compositional, and technical explanations.

Regulatory dependencies

Prioritize regulators with evidence for a selective vulnerability and a clear experimental test.

Tissue and clinical context

Evaluate cellular programs alongside microenvironmental context and established clinical predictors.

My independent direction rests on the questions, comparative framework, software, and evidence standards that connect these aims. It builds on collaborative research without assuming exclusive ownership of institutional datasets.
