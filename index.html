Syed Abbas Bukhari, PhD

Computational systems biologist studying cancer gene regulation and tumor microenvironments.

Staff Scientist · Oncogenomics Laboratory · National Cancer Institute (NCI), NIH

I integrate bulk, single-cell, and spatial transcriptomics with gene-regulatory network (GRN) modeling to identify lineage programs, prognostic cell states, and actionable dependencies in pediatric solid tumors. My work bridges systems biology and translational oncology — using regulatory and microenvironment-aware models to prioritize therapeutic targets and stratify patients.

Before NCI, I trained in neurogenomics and comparative systems biology at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, studying conserved transcriptional programs underlying social behavior across vertebrates and invertebrates. That background informs how I think about regulatory architecture and cell-state plasticity in cancer today.
