A coherent research trajectory

My research follows one question across biological scales: how does molecular regulation produce differences in phenotype? During my doctoral work, I integrated temporal transcription and chromatin evidence. Postdoctoral work extended that perspective across species and into single-cell regulatory networks.

At NCI, I apply these principles to pediatric tumor states and candidate dependencies. I bring both scientific ownership and hands-on implementation: defining a question, building the analysis, evaluating uncertainty, and communicating what the results mean for the next decision.
