About & CV

I am a computational systems biologist studying how molecular regulation contributes to differences among cells, organisms, and disease states.

My research has moved from sequence prediction and structural evolution to neurogenomics and pediatric oncology. Across these systems, I combine computational analysis with biological interpretation and explicit evaluation of uncertainty.
