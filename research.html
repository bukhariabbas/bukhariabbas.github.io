Research

My research applies computational and systems-level approaches to understand gene regulation, cell-state dynamics, and microenvironment composition in cancer and other complex traits.

Tumor Microenvironment Inference in Rhabdomyosarcoma

Using a single-cell RMS reference atlas, I deconvolute bulk RNA-seq from patient cohorts to estimate cell-type and cell-state proportions, then test associations between inferred composition and clinical outcomes to define prognostic clusters. The work aims to build TME-aware risk models that complement existing molecular classifiers.

Detailed pipeline and supplementary reports available on request.

Pan-Pediatric Gene-Regulatory Networks & Target Prioritization

I build consensus regulatory networks across pediatric tumor types using ARACNe-AP and GENIE3, quantify regulon activity through differential-expression enrichment, and integrate the results with DepMap dependencies and drug resources (PRISM, ChEMBL, DrugBank, MIPE) to nominate context-specific therapeutic targets.

Single-Cell, Spatial, and CITE-seq Workflows

Day-to-day I work across the full transcriptomics stack: QC, integration, annotation, trajectory and lineage analysis, spatial niche analysis, and surface-protein/transcript joint modeling. These workflows feed both discovery projects and the deconvolution / GRN pipelines above.

Comparative Neurogenomics & Systems Biology

Earlier work (PhD/postdoc, UIUC) examined conserved neurogenomic responses to social interaction across sticklebacks, honey bees, and mice — establishing how I think about regulatory program conservation, cell-state dynamics, and meta-analytic integration today.
